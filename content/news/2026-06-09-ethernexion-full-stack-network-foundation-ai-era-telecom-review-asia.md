Great to see Telecom Review Asia covering one of the most important shifts happening in our industry today—the transformation of network infrastructure in the AI era.

As AI, hyperscale data centers, and edge computing accelerate across APAC, network design is being fundamentally redefined. This isn’t just about scaling bandwidth anymore. It’s about delivering deterministic performance, ultra-low latency, and intelligent coordination between compute and network resources.

What resonates most in this piece is the idea of the network evolving into a “Neural Hub”—not just moving data, but actively participating in compute workflows. From lossless 400G/800G fabrics to AI-driven automation and application-aware traffic orchestration, we’re seeing a clear shift toward networks that are built for AI from the ground up.

At the same time, simplicity is becoming a competitive advantage. In increasingly complex environments, unified operating systems, cloud-managed architectures, and silicon-level optimization are critical to reducing TCO while maintaining performance at scale.

If you’re thinking about how AI workloads will reshape your infrastructure strategy, this is well worth the read. It offers a practical look at where network architecture is heading—and why the future will favor those who can align performance, intelligence, and efficiency in one cohesive stack.
