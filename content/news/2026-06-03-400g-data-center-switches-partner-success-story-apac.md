### Product Introduction

The S9300 series switch is a high-performance and high-density switch built on the cutting-edge 25.6T chip, with a single port supporting 400G transmission capability. It supports the pluggable power supply and fan, making it flexible and more efficient in adapting to various large data center scenarios.

1\. Support MLAG and cross device link aggregation technology

2\. Support Precision Timing Protocol

3\. Support VxLAN and BGP protocols

4\. Supports IPv6, OSPF, BGP, PIM, etc

### Hardware Form

- Ethernet Ports: 32×400G QSFP112
- Management: 1 x RJ45 ETH Managed Port
- Fan Modules: 6 x pluggable fan modules
- Power Supply: 2 x 1300W pluggable power supply

### Operation Management

The S9300 series switches support a variety of management interfaces, including Console, internal and external network ports, and USB ports; Supports SNMP v1/v2/v3, CLI command line, web network management, TELNET and FTP configuration, OAM and SSH 2.0, SSL and other encryption methods, making device management more convenient and secure.
