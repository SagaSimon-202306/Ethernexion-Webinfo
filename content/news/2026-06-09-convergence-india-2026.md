### Bridging the Value Gap: From 1G to 800G AI-Ready Networks

![](https://www.ethernexion.com:8001/media/original_images/welcome-convergence.webp)

**NEW DELHI, INDIA** – Ethernexion, a leader in high-performance Ethernet switch solutions, is proud to announce its participation in the 33rd edition of **Convergence India**.

As the networking industry pivots toward AI-driven architectures and Cloud-native infrastructure, Ethernexion is positioning itself as the strategic partner of choice for those seeking Tier-1 performance without the legacy price premium.

### What We’re Showcasing:

- **The Power of One:** A live demonstration of the **Unified ENOS (Operating System)**, showing how a single software platform can manage everything from 1G access to 800G cores.
- **WiFi 7 Excellence:** Our latest wireless series, delivering 3600Mbps throughput for high-density environments.
- **Supply Chain Confidence:** Insights into our diversified manufacturing hubs in Thailand and the Philippines, ensuring global delivery resilience.
- **The AI Data Center Frontier:** Our **S8 Series (100G QSFP28)** switches, featuring ultra-low latency and lossless networking for GPU clusters.

**Meet Our Team** Our technical and business experts, led by **Andy**, will be on-site to discuss how our “Value Gap” strategy can optimize your network TCO and accelerate your digital transformation.
