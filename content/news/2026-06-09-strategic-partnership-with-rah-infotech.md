Ethernexion is pleased to announce a strategic partnership with RAH Infotech, which will serve as our authorized Value-Added Distributor (VAD) in India. This is asignificant step in our effort to expand across one of the world’s fastest-growing digital markets. It also reflects our commitment to delivering high-performance, reliable networking solutions that are tailored specifically to the needs of Indian businesses.

India is undergoing rapid digital transformation. From cloud adoption and AI-driven applications to the rise of hyperscale data centers and enterprise networks,the demand for fast, scalable, and resilient connectivity has never been greater. At Ethernexion, we see this as both an opportunity and a responsibility — to help build the infrastructure that enables innovation at scale.

“Abdul Rahman, Managing Director of Ethernexion Singapore: We are delighted topartner with RAH Infotech, a leading technology distributor renowned for its expertise and innovative solutions. Their 20-year legacy of empowering businesses with seamless connectivity, robust security, and flexible infrastructure makes them an ideal partner for us. This collaboration allows us to bring Ethernexion’s high-performance networking solutions closer to enterprises across India.

Together, we look forward to supporting organizations in adopting next-generation networks with confidence, accelerating digital transformation, and exploring new opportunities for growth and innovation in this dynamic market.”

Ethernexion’s portfolio of network switching solutions ranges from 1G to 800G, covering environments from campus and enterprise networks to telecom edge and hyperscale data centers. Our products are built on continuous innovation in high-speed signal processing, high-density PCB design, and unified software platforms — delivering performance, efficiency, and long-term value to our customers.

With over 20 years of experience, RAH Infotech is a well-established technology distributor with deep expertise in networking, cybersecurity, cloud, and application performance. By combining Ethernexion’s advanced solutions with RAH’s local market knowledge and extensive channel network, this partnership brings technology and support closer to customers across India. It’s easier for businesses to confidently adopt next-generation networking architectures.

This partnership is built on shared values: openness, collaboration, and a commitment to long-term success. Together, Ethernexion and RAH Infotech aim to deliver real value — cutting-edge networking solutions, accessible local support, and the flexibility enterprises need to grow and innovate.

At Ethernexion, we believe great networks are built together. Our collaboration with RAH Infotech marks a new chapter in India’s digital journey — one where high-performance connectivity is paired with local insight and customer-focused service.

We look forward to seeing the impact this partnership will have on enterprises and service providers across the region, helping them navigate the next phase of digital growth with speed, confidence, and clarity.
