# Ethernexion All-in-One Network Solution for SMBs

An all-in-one, secure, cloud-managed network solution tailored for SMBs.

[Explore Products](https://test.hohunet.cn/products/switches/collections/enos-data-center-switches) [Request Support](https://test.hohunet.cn/support)

[Contact](https://www.ethernexion.com/solutions/ethernexion-all-in-one-network-solution-for-smbs#contact)

Amid the wave of digital transformation, SMBs have become more reliant on networks than ever before. Whether for day-to-day office collaboration, high-definition video conferencing, or cloud-based synchronization of critical business data, networks have long been the lifeline of these businesses. However, traditional SMB network infrastructure often faces certain challenges.

![](https://www.ethernexion.com:8001/media/original_images/SMBS.webp)

- ![](https://www.ethernexion.com:8001/media/original_images/YunYingChengBenFenXi.png)

### High O&M Costs

With no dedicated IT staff, troubleshooting network issues is difficult, and any network outage brings operations to a halt.

- ![](https://www.ethernexion.com:8001/media/original_images/SheBeiGuZhang.png)

### Poor Device Synergy

A patchwork of hardware from various vendors lacks unified management, preventing coordination between gateways, switches, and APs.

- ![](https://www.ethernexion.com:8001/media/original_images/HuLianWang_SheBeiLianJie_YuanChengXieZhu.png)

### Limited Scalability

The network architecture cannot be smoothly upgraded to accommodate business growth or office expansion.

- ![](https://www.ethernexion.com:8001/media/original_images/TongXinAnQuanLouDong_1.png)

### Severe Security Vulnerabilities

The lack of effective visitor isolation and security measures for both internal and external networks makes the system vulnerable to cyberattacks.

Supports simultaneous connections from multi-broadband lines with intelligent traffic distribution; automatically switches to an alternative line within seconds if one fails, ensuring business continuity.

Accurately identifies workplace and entertainment apps, allowing employers to restrict P2P downloads or non-work streaming with one click, prioritizing bandwidth for video calls and ERP systems.

Provides robust defense against DoS/DDoS attacks and supports one-click VPN tunnel establishment for seamless branch connectivity.

Delivers high-power output per port to directly fuel APs, IP phones, and surveillance cameras, completely simplifying office cabling.

### Audio/Video Traffic Prioritization

Built-in smart QoS strategies automatically identify and prioritize voice and video traffic, eliminating lagging during crucial meetings.

### Enterprise-Grade High Reliability

Powered by an industry-leading switching chip architecture with loop protection, preventing total network paralysis even if a stray cable is misplugged.

Supports 802.11k/v/r roaming protocols. As employees move between conference rooms, workstations, and the front desk, the network automatically switches within seconds, ensuring uninterrupted video calls.

Supports hundreds of concurrent client connections per AP, easily handling high density meeting scenarios.

### Secure Guest Isolation

One-click deployment of an exclusive guest Wi-Fi network that is physically isolated from internal corporate resources (NAS, servers), protecting your confidential data.
