## S5300 系列各页共用 Highlights（逐字；PoE 型号含最后一条）

- Support 5G Multi-Gigatbit ports and various uplink ports to meet different network requirements.

- Rich L2/L3 features such as VLAN,IGMP Snooping,LACP,OSPF,VRRP,ACL,DHCP etc.

- Support VxLAN and BGP protocols to build EVPN applications in the data center scenario.

- Support the use of MACsec or AES-CMAC for encryption between switches.

- Support the Precision Timing protocol.

- PoE++ provide max 90W power of ports.

Lossless Network Features

- Supports MLAG
- Supports PFC/ECN
- Supports relay and access port modes
- Supports VLAN registration (GVRP/MVRP)
- Supports Generalized Routing Encapsulation Protocol (GRE)

- Support IS-IS
- Support routing interfaces

- Support equal-cost multipath routing (ECMP)
- Support PBR (policy-based routing)
- Support virtual router redundancy protocol (VRRP)
- Support DHCP (listening/relaying for IPv4 and IPv6)
- Support IGMP v2/v3
- Support PIM SM/SSM
- Support BFD
- Support dual-stack of IPv4/IPv6
