## S5100 系列各页共用 Highlights（逐字）

- Supports MLAG (Multi-Layer Aggregation Link),a cross-device link aggregation technology,achieving device-level redundancy,flexible networking with strong reliability. Support VxLAN and BGP protocols to build EVPN applications in the data center scenario.

- Support the use of MACsec or AES-CMAC for encryption between switches.

- Support the Precision Timing protocol.

- Rich L2/L3 features,such as IPv6,OSPF,BGP,PIM,etc.

- Precision Timing Protocol.
