## S5200 系列各页共用 Highlights（逐字，PoE 型号含最后两条，非 PoE 型号不含 "PoE++ provide max 90W power of ports."）

- Supports MLAG (Multi-Link Aggregation) and cross-device link aggregation technology,achieving device-level redundancy and enabling flexible networking with strong reliability.

- Support VxLAN and BGP protocols to build EVPN applications in the data center scenario.

- Support the use of MACsec or AES-CMAC for encryption between switches.

- Support the Precision Timing protocol.

- Rich L2/L3 features,such as IPv6,OSPF,BGP,PIM,etc.

- 5G downlink ports and various uplink ports to meet different network requirements.

- PoE++ provide max 90W power of ports.
