# SONiC Commercialization Solutions

From silicon to source code, our ZEGMA-powered reference platform slashes your commercialization time by 80%.

With the explosive growth of cloud computing, large AI models, and edge computing, traditional “black-box” switches, due to their closed software ecosystems are gradually becoming a bottleneck for enterprises seeking rapid deployment and flexible scalability.

SONiC (Software for Open Networking in the Cloud), as the world’s leading open-source networking operating system, has become the cornerstone for building next-generation data center networks thanks to its decoupled, modular, and highly scalable characteristics.

However, transforming the open-source SONiC into a stable, commercially viable product still faces significant challenges, including difficulties in hardware adaptation, the complexity of debugging the SAI (Switch Abstraction Interface) layer, and long R&D cycles.

## A dual-engine model combining hardware platforms and barebone system source code

Building on years of deep expertise in switch R&D, Ethernexion has launched an integrated solution featuring the ZEGMA chip hardware platform and the SONiC barebone reference design.

We don’t just sell hardware; we provide you with a ready-to-use foundation for your R&D. By abstracting away the complexity of the underlying hardware, we enable your R&D team to bypass tedious low-level adaptation and move directly to developing higher-level business logic and value-added features, significantly shortening the time-to-market from project initiation to launch.
