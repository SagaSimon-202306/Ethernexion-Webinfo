# Cookie policy

This site may use essential cookies required for security and basic functionality. Analytics or marketing cookies, if enabled, will be described here when deployed.

## Server logs and form submissions

When you submit a contact form or subscribe to our newsletter, we record your IP address in server logs and in our content management system for abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and audit purposes. These records are not used for advertising profiling. Retention follows our internal log and database policies.
