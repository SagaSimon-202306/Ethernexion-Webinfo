# About EtherNexion

Build high-performance network infrastructure for the AI era

ETHERNEXION is a Singapore company, Founded in 2024, it is a technology-driven pioneer in Ethernet switch research and development. Utilizing a unified hardware platform and software architecture, the company delivers a versatile portfolio of high-performance switches ranging from 1G to 800G.
ETHERNEXION products power critical infrastructure across enterprise networks, carrier networks, data centers, and AI computing hubs. Through continuous hardware innovation and agile software iteration, the company prioritizes reliability, ease of use, and seamless maintainability to drive ongoing value for global customers.

## How We Work

Empowering Partners, Elevating Localized Excellence.

Ethernexion operates a partner-centric business model. As a high-performance communication products manufacturer, we expand our global reach and deliver exceptional localized service exclusively through our ecosystem of registered partners.

## Our People

At Ethernexion, our greatest strength is our people. We are a collective of creators and innovators united by a single mission: to redefine what network infrastructure can be.

With deep expertise in data networking, embedded systems, and high‑speed switching architectures, our engineers anticipate market challenges. From chip‑level efficiency to resilient protocols, we turn complex technical obstacles into market-leading solutions.

We listen first, design second. By collaborating closely with global customers across enterprise, industrial, and cloud environments, we ensure every feature and line of code delivers real-world performance, security, and scalability. We build products with our customers, not just for them.

Inclusive & Forward-Thinking: We believe great innovation grows from diverse minds. Our global culture fosters curiosity, continuous learning, and shared excellence, keeping our team at the cutting edge of networking technology.

At Ethernexion, we don’t just build products—we build trust, connectivity, and the foundation for tomorrow’s digital world.
